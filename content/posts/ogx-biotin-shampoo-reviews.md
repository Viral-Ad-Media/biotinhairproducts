> Formulas change. This review is based on how the brand describes the product, so always check the current ingredient list on the bottle before you buy.

OGX Thick & Full Biotin & Collagen is probably the shampoo most people picture when they hear "biotin shampoo." It's cheap, widely available, and built for fine hair that falls flat.

![OGX Biotin & Collagen shampoo](/uploads/2022/02/ogx-shampoo.jpg)

## What's in it

OGX describes the formula as a blend of biotin, collagen and hydrolyzed wheat protein, made with sulfate-free surfactants. It has OGX's signature strong, sweet fragrance.

## Who it's for

- Fine or thin hair that needs lift at the root
- Anyone wanting an affordable, easy-to-find thickening shampoo
- People who like a noticeable scent

## Pros

- Gives fine hair visible body after the first wash
- Inexpensive and stocked almost everywhere
- Matching conditioner and leave-in products

## Cons

- The fragrance can bother sensitive scalps
- Contains wheat protein, so skip it if you're sensitive to wheat
- Can feel a little drying on very curly or coarse hair

## How to use it

Massage into a wet scalp for about a minute, rinse thoroughly, then use conditioner on the mid-lengths and ends only so you don't flatten the roots.

## Verdict

OGX Biotin & Collagen is a solid, budget-friendly pick for fine hair that needs volume. It won't regrow hair, but it reliably makes hair look fuller. Patch test first if your scalp is sensitive.
