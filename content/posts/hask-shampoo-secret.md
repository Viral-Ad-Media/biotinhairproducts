> Formulas change. This review is based on how the brand describes the product, so always check the current ingredient list on the bottle before you buy.

HASK is a drugstore brand known for its colorful bottles and single-use hair treatment packets. Its Biotin Boost Thickening Shampoo is aimed at fine, limp hair.

![HASK Biotin Boost shampoo](/uploads/2022/03/hask-shampoo-1.jpg)

## What's in it

HASK describes Biotin Boost as a sulfate-free formula with biotin, collagen and coffee, and says it's free of parabens and phthalates.

## Who it's for

- Fine or thin hair that needs volume
- Color-treated hair (sulfate-free formulas are gentler on color)
- Shoppers who want an affordable sulfate-free option

## Pros

- Sulfate-free at a drugstore price
- Adds body without leaving hair stiff
- Widely available with matching conditioner and treatments

## Cons

- Sulfate-free formulas lather less, which some people dislike
- Fragrance may not suit sensitive scalps
- May not cut through heavy build-up on its own

## How to use it

Wet hair thoroughly so the lower-lather formula spreads well. Massage into the scalp, rinse, and condition the ends. Use a clarifying shampoo now and then if you use lots of styling products.

## Verdict

HASK Biotin Boost is one of the best-value sulfate-free biotin shampoos. It's a sensible choice for fine or color-treated hair that needs a lift.
