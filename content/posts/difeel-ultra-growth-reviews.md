> Formulas change. This review is based on how the brand describes the product, so always check the current ingredient list on the bottle before you buy.

Difeel is known for affordable hair oils, and its Ultra Growth line extends that into shampoos and conditioners aimed at stronger, fuller-looking hair.

![Difeel Ultra Growth shampoo](/uploads/2022/02/difeel-ultra-growth.jpg)

## What's in it

Difeel describes its Ultra Growth products as combining biotin with botanical oils, such as castor and basil, depending on the specific product. Check the bottle for the exact formula, since the line has several versions.

## Who it's for

- Dry, coarse or curly hair that likes oils
- People already using Difeel's hair oils who want a matching routine
- Budget-conscious shoppers

## Pros

- Affordable
- Oil-rich formulas can leave hair soft and shiny
- Works well alongside the brand's growth oils

## Cons

- May weigh down fine or oily hair
- Strong scent for some users
- "Growth" branding oversells what any shampoo can do

## How to use it

Focus the shampoo on the scalp and let the suds run through the lengths. If your hair is fine, use a lightweight conditioner afterwards rather than the richer matching one.

## Verdict

Difeel Ultra Growth suits people with drier, thicker hair who want softness and shine on a budget. It's a pleasant routine, but treat the "growth" promise as marketing.
