Hair grows about a centimeter a month, and most of what affects that is happening inside your body. These tips focus on what you can actually control.

![Stages of hair loss](/uploads/2022/04/hair-loss-stages.jpg)

## 1. Eat enough protein and iron

Hair is made of keratin, a protein. Diets low in protein or iron are among the most common reasons for thinning, especially in women. Eggs, fish, beans, lentils, lean meat and leafy greens all help.

## 2. Don't overlook other nutrients

Vitamin D, zinc and B vitamins (including biotin) all play a part in healthy hair. A balanced diet covers most people. If you suspect a deficiency, ask your doctor for a blood test rather than guessing with high-dose supplements.

## 3. Be gentle with your hair

- Avoid tight ponytails, braids and extensions that pull at the roots.
- Turn down the heat on straighteners and dryers, and use a heat protectant.
- Detangle wet hair gently with a wide-toothed comb.
- Space out bleaching, perms and relaxers.

## 4. Look after your scalp

A clean, healthy scalp gives hair the best start. Wash often enough to clear oil and product build-up, and treat dandruff if you have it.

## 5. Use a good biotin shampoo

A biotin shampoo won't change your genes, but a well-made one cleans gently, adds body and reduces breakage, so you keep more of the length you grow. Massage it into the scalp for a minute each wash.

## 6. Manage stress and sleep

A stressful event, illness or major change can push many hairs into the resting phase at once, causing heavy shedding two to three months later. It usually recovers on its own, and good sleep and stress management help.

## 7. Know when to see a doctor

See a doctor or dermatologist if you notice:

- Sudden or patchy hair loss
- A widening part or receding hairline
- Shedding with other symptoms such as tiredness or weight changes

Treatments like minoxidil and others are far more effective for pattern hair loss than any shampoo, and they work best when started early.

![Restored, healthy hair](/uploads/2022/03/restored-hair-1.jpg)

## The bottom line

Healthy hair comes from good nutrition, gentle care and a healthy scalp. A biotin shampoo is a helpful part of that routine, not a replacement for it.
