> Formulas change. This review is based on how the brand describes the product, so always check the current ingredient list on the bottle before you buy.

Renpure is a budget brand known for large bottles of salon-style formulas. Its Biotin & Collagen Thickening Shampoo is one of its best sellers.

![Renpure Biotin & Collagen shampoo](/uploads/2022/02/renpure-shampoo.jpg)

## What's in it

Renpure describes the formula as biotin and collagen in a sulfate-free, paraben-free base designed to make hair look thicker and fuller.

## Who it's for

- Fine or normal hair that needs body
- Families or households who want one large, affordable bottle
- People trying their first biotin shampoo

## Pros

- Excellent value per ounce
- Gentle, sulfate-free cleansing
- Easy to find in supermarkets and pharmacies

## Cons

- Large bottles take a long time to finish, so check the expiry date
- Scent and texture may vary between batches
- Not moisturizing enough on its own for very dry or coily hair

## How to use it

Massage into the scalp, rinse and follow with conditioner. For dry hair, pair it with a richer conditioner or mask.

![Thick, healthy hair](/uploads/2022/03/thick-black-hair.png)

## Verdict

Renpure Biotin & Collagen is a dependable, low-cost thickening shampoo. If you want to try a biotin shampoo without spending much, it's a good place to start.
