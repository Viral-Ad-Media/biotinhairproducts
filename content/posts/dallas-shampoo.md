> Formulas change. This review is based on how the brand describes the product, so always check the current ingredient list on the bottle before you buy.

DALLAS Biotin Thickening Shampoo is sold mainly online and is marketed to people who want thicker-looking hair, often with a matching conditioner.

![DALLAS Biotin Thickening shampoo](/uploads/2022/02/Dallas-shampoo.jpg)

## What's in it

The brand markets it as a biotin-based thickening shampoo combined with botanical extracts and oils. Check the listing for the full ingredient list, since online-only brands sometimes update formulas without much notice.

## Who it's for

- Fine or thinning hair that needs body
- Shoppers who buy most of their hair care online
- People who want a shampoo and conditioner set

## Pros

- Aimed squarely at volume and thickness
- Usually good value for the size
- Often sold as a set with conditioner

## Cons

- Mainly available online, so counterfeits and old stock are a risk
- Botanical extracts and fragrance may irritate sensitive scalps
- Less independent information about it than for big drugstore brands

## How to use it

Use it like any volumizing shampoo: work it into the scalp, leave for a minute, rinse well and condition the ends. Buy only from the brand's official store, and check the seal and expiry date. Our guide to [where to buy biotin shampoo](/where-to-buy-biotin-shampoo-for-hair) explains how.

## Verdict

DALLAS Biotin is a reasonable choice if you want an online thickening shampoo and can buy from the official seller. As with every biotin shampoo, expect fuller-looking hair, not regrowth.
