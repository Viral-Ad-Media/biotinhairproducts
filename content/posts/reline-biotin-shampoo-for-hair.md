> Formulas change. This review is based on how the brand describes the product, so always check the current ingredient list on the bottle before you buy.

Reline is a smaller brand sold mostly through online stores, marketed as a biotin shampoo for thinning hair.

![Reline biotin shampoo](/uploads/2022/03/reline-shampoo.jpg)

## What's in it

Reline markets the shampoo around biotin combined with plant extracts and oils. Because it's a smaller brand, information can be harder to find, so read the full ingredient list on the listing or bottle before you buy.

## Who it's for

- People who want to try a smaller-brand biotin shampoo
- Fine or thinning hair looking for more body
- Those who also buy the matching conditioner

## Pros

- Focused on thinning, flat hair
- Often sold as a shampoo and conditioner pair
- Usually priced competitively

## Cons

- Less independent information than for well-known brands
- Sold mainly online, where fakes and old stock are a risk
- Plant extracts and fragrance can irritate sensitive scalps

## How to use it

Patch test first, especially with less familiar brands. Then use as normal: massage into the scalp, rinse well, and condition the ends.

![Reline shampoo and conditioner](/uploads/2022/02/reline-shampoo-and-conditioner.jpg)

## Verdict

Reline may suit you if you like trying smaller brands, but buy only from the official seller and check the bottle on arrival. If you'd rather stick with known quantities, OGX, HASK and Renpure are easier to find.
