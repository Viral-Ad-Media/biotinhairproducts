> Formulas change. This review is based on how the brand describes the product, so always check the current ingredient list on the bottle before you buy.

Honeydew is an online-first brand that sells biotin shampoo and conditioner aimed at people with thinning or fine hair.

![Honeydew biotin shampoo](/uploads/2022/03/honeydew-biotin-shampoo.jpg)

## What's in it

Honeydew markets its shampoo as biotin combined with plant oils and botanical extracts in a sulfate-free formula. Check the current label for the full list.

## Who it's for

- Fine or thinning hair that needs volume
- People wanting a sulfate-free option sold online
- Those who like a shampoo and conditioner set

## Pros

- Sulfate-free and gentle on color
- Leaves hair soft
- Often good value as a set

## Cons

- Mostly available online, so buy from the official store
- Oils can weigh down very fine hair if you over-apply
- Fragrance may bother sensitive scalps

## How to use it

Use a small amount, massage into the scalp and rinse well. If your hair feels heavy, shampoo twice or use less conditioner.

![Healthy hair after washing](/uploads/2022/03/honeydew.jpg)

## Verdict

Honeydew is a gentle, pleasant biotin shampoo for people who shop online. Expect softer, fuller-looking hair, not regrowth.
