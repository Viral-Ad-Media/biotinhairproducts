> Formulas change. This review is based on how the brand describes the product, so always check the current ingredient list on the bottle before you buy.

Briogeo is a premium hair care brand known for "clean" formulas. Its Blossom & Bloom Ginseng + Biotin Volumizing Shampoo is aimed at fine, thinning hair.

![Briogeo Blossom & Bloom shampoo](/uploads/2022/02/brioeo-blossom-and-bloom-shampoo.jpg)

## What's in it

Briogeo describes the formula as combining ginseng, biotin and ginger to boost volume and support a healthy scalp. The brand says its products are made without sulfates, silicones, parabens and phthalates.

## Who it's for

- Fine, flat or thinning hair
- Shoppers who prefer clean-label formulas
- People happy to pay more for a salon-style product

## Pros

- Lightweight and volumizing without heavy silicones
- Free of many ingredients people try to avoid
- Pleasant, fresh scent

## Cons

- Much more expensive than drugstore options
- Fragrance and botanical extracts may still irritate very sensitive skin
- Gentle cleansers may not be enough for heavy product build-up

## How to use it

Apply to wet hair and massage the scalp. If you use a lot of styling product, clarify once a week or wash twice.

![Before and after using a volumizing shampoo](/uploads/2022/02/before-and-after-use-of-briogeo-shampoo.jpg)

## Verdict

Briogeo Blossom & Bloom is one of the nicer volumizing shampoos if your budget allows and you value a clean formula. The results are cosmetic, like any biotin shampoo, but they're pleasant.
