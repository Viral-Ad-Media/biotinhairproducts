Biotin shampoos are some of the most popular "thickening" products on drugstore shelves. Before you buy one, it helps to know what the benefits actually are, and which ones come from the biotin itself versus the rest of the formula.

## What biotin is

Biotin is vitamin B7. Your body uses it to turn food into energy and to build keratin, the protein your hair, skin and nails are made of. Most people get enough from food such as eggs, salmon, nuts, seeds and sweet potatoes, and true biotin deficiency is rare.

## The realistic benefits

### 1. Hair that looks and feels fuller

Most biotin shampoos are built as volumizing formulas. Light cleansers, proteins such as hydrolyzed keratin or collagen, and fewer heavy silicones help each strand stand away from the scalp. The result is hair that looks thicker right after washing, even though the number of hairs hasn't changed.

### 2. Less breakage

Proteins and conditioning agents smooth the cuticle, the outer layer of each hair. Smoother hair tangles less and snaps less when you brush it, so you lose fewer strands to breakage over time. That can make hair look longer and denser.

### 3. A clean, comfortable scalp

Hair grows best from a scalp that isn't clogged with oil, product build-up or flakes. A gentle shampoo used regularly keeps follicles clear. Many biotin shampoos also include soothing extras like aloe or panthenol.

### 4. A good base for a wider routine

A biotin shampoo works best as one part of a routine that also includes a good diet, gentle styling and, if needed, a conversation with a doctor about the cause of any shedding.

## What biotin shampoo can't do

There's little evidence that biotin rubbed on the scalp is absorbed in a way that makes new hair grow. Biotin supplements help hair mainly in people who are actually low in biotin. If you're losing hair in patches, shedding suddenly, or noticing a widening part, see a doctor or dermatologist, because thyroid problems, iron deficiency, stress and genetics are far more common causes.

## How to get the most from it

- Massage it into the scalp for a minute rather than just the lengths.
- Rinse well and follow with a lightweight conditioner on the ends.
- Give it four to six weeks before judging the results.
- Skip very hot water and heavy heat styling, which undo the benefits.

## The bottom line

A good biotin shampoo can make thin hair look fuller and break less. Treat it as a cosmetic boost, not a cure for hair loss, and you'll be happy with it.
